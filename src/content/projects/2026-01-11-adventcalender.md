I created an advent calendar as a gift for a friend. The only requirement was that it should contain small chocolate pieces, while the design of the calendar itself was completely up to me. I had wanted to try laser cutting for a long time, so this felt like the perfect opportunity to do so.

I started by designing the calendar in Fusion 360. After some research, I decided that the calendar should take the shape of a tree with a star on top. The star is represented by an electric candle, placed on a small platform between the two wooden panels. Star-shaped cutouts were added to the panels to create a decorative pattern and let light through.

![](/images/projects/Adventskalender/Skjermbilde%202026-01-10%20kl.%2020.13.05.png)

Next, I began the production process, starting with the drawers that hold the chocolate, followed by the main wooden frame that forms the tree structure.

![](/images/projects/Adventskalender/Skuffer.jpg)

![](/images/projects/Adventskalender/Laserkutte.jpg)

The tree was assembled using screws with spacers, which hold the two wooden panels apart.

![](/images/projects/Adventskalender/Uten%20fyll.jpg)

Finally, everything was put together. I added a cotton string between the spacers to prevent visibility into the drawers. This detail also enhances the tree-like appearance and adds a soft, decorative finish to the overall design.
