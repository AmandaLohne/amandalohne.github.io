Recently, I have spent a lot of my time working with ceramics, a craft I have grown very fond of. It is a time-consuming process filled with both challenges and rewarding moments, which has made it especially meaningful to me.

![](/images/projects/Ceramics/Idyll.jpg)

Over the years, I have created a large number of ceramic pieces, and I sell selected works through Tise ([https://tise.com/amandaloh](https://tise.com/amandaloh)). This allows me to share my work with others while continuing to develop my skills.

![](/images/projects/Ceramics/Hylle.jpg)

Below are some of the projects I have worked on. These pieces represent more challenging and experimental processes—projects that push my abilities and inspire further exploration.

This cup resembles a climbing wall, with the handle shaped like a climbing hold.

![](/images/projects/Ceramics/Buldrekopp.jpg)

This cup features a handle that appears difficult to use, but is designed to be surprisingly comfortable and enjoyable to hold.
