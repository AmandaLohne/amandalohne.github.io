I wanted to find a way to conceal the laundry basket while also making better use of the space above it. This led to the idea of creating a table that could both hide the basket underneath and function as a practical surface on top.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Uten_bord.jpg)

The design was created in Fusion 360. To ensure the table matched the existing bamboo elements in the bathroom, bamboo was also chosen as the material for the laundry table.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Skjermbilde%202026-01-11%20kl.%2019.09.08.png)

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Farge%202.png)

An existing bamboo piece of furniture was cut into evenly sized squares, which were then glued onto a base plate to form the tabletop. The edges were routed afterward to ensure a clean, flush finish.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Pusslespill%203.jpeg)

The edges were routed afterward to ensure a clean, flush finish.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Flush.jpeg)

To improve usability, wheels were added to the laundry basket, making it easy to slide in and out from under the table. A custom mount for the wheels was 3D printed to securely attach them to the basket’s frame.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Koblinga%20ferdig.jpeg)

Once everything was assembled, the table was complete—combining functionality, material consistency, and a clean, integrated design.

![](/images/projects/Skittentoy/Koblinga%20ferdig%20bilde.jpeg)
