I wanted to make a hanging plant a more integrated part of the interior, which led to the idea of transforming it into a climbing plant. To achieve this, I needed a visually pleasing way to attach the plant to the wall. I decided that 3D printing could be a good solution.

After a quick search online, I found many different design approaches. I was particularly drawn to designs shaped like leaves. This inspired me to create a leaf-shaped clip that gently holds the plant against the wall while blending naturally into the surroundings.

![](/images/projects/Planteklips/Hengeplante%20bilde.jpg)

The clip was designed in Fusion 360. A leaf pattern was added to the outward-facing side to enhance the aesthetic, while careful consideration was given to the size of the opening to ensure the plant would not be damaged.

![](/images/projects/Planteklips/Skjermbilde%202026-01-11%20kl.%2019.53.33.png)

The locking mechanism consists of two parts that slide together, providing a simple and secure hold.

![](/images/projects/Planteklips/Planteklips%20svarte%202.png)

Once printed, the clips were mounted on the wall using double-sided tape. The design is also available on Printables: [https://www.printables.com/model/349056-plant-clip.](https://www.printables.com/model/349056-plant-clip)
